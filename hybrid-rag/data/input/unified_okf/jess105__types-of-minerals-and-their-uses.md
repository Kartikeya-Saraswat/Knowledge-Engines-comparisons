# Types of Minerals and Their Uses

Minerals are classified based on their mode of occurrence and properties. For example, ferrous minerals are important for metallurgical industries, and they are found in places like Odisha, Chhattisgarh, and Jharkhand. Non-ferrous minerals, such as copper and zinc, are also important and are found in various regions of India.
