# Crop Patterns and Seasons in India

India has three cropping seasons: rabi, kharif, and zaid. Rabi crops are sown in winter and harvested in summer, with important crops like wheat, barley, and mustard. Kharif crops are grown with the onset of monsoon and include paddy, maize, and cotton. The zaid season is a short summer season with crops like watermelon and muskmelon. Major crops include rice, wheat, millets, pulses, and tea.
