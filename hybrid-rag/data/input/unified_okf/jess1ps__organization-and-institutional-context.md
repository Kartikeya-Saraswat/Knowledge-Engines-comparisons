# Organization and Institutional Context

The National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) is an organization committed to systemic reform and continuous improvement in the quality of its products. It welcomes comments and suggestions to further refine its publications. The organization is based in New Delhi and was established on 20 November 2006.
