# Non-Conventional Energy Sources

India uses non-conventional energy sources like solar, wind, tidal, and biogas to reduce dependence on fossil fuels. Solar energy is tapped in tropical regions, wind power is harnessed in states like Tamil Nadu and Gujarat, tidal energy is used in the Gulf of Khambhat and Gulf of Kuchchh, and biogas is produced from organic waste in rural areas.
