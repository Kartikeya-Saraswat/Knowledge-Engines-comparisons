# Types of Soil and Their Features

Black soil is predominantly found in Uttarakhand and Maharashtra. It has high water-retaining capacity and is suitable for agriculture. It is intensively leached in the monsoon climate. Terraces in the Western and central Himalayas are made of these soils. Strip cropping and shelter belts help prevent erosion. The Great Plains of India are made up of these soils.
