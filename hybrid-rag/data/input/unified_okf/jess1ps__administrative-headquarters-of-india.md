# Administrative Headquarters of India

The administrative headquarters of Chandigarh, Haryana and Punjab are at Chandigarh. The state boundaries between Uttarakhand & Uttar Pradesh, Bihar & Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh & Madhya Pradesh have not been verified by the Governments concerned.
