# Mica Deposits and Producers

Mica deposits are found in the northern edge of the Chota Nagpur plateau. Koder ma Gaya – Hazaribagh belt of Jharkhand is the leading producer. In Rajasthan, the major mica producing area is around Ajmer. Nellor e mica belt of Andhra Pradesh is also an important producer in the country.
