# Major Sea Ports in India

Deendayal Port (Kandla) in Kuchchh - first port developed after Independence
Deendayal Port (Mumbai) - largest port with natural and well-sheltered harbour
Mormugao port (Goa) - premier iron ore exporting port
New Mangalore port - caters to export of iron ore concentrates
Cochin port - extreme south-western port with natural harbour
