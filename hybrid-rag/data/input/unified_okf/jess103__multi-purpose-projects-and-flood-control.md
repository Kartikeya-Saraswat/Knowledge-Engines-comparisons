# Multi-Purpose Projects and Flood Control

The Sardar Sarovar Dam is built over the Narmada River in Gujarat and provides irrigation to 18.45 lakh hectares in 15 districts. It also irrigates land in desert and tribal regions through lift irrigation. The project aims to make drought-prone areas drought-proof with assured water supply.
