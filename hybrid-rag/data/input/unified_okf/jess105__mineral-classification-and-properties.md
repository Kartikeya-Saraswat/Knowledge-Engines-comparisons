# Mineral Classification and Properties

Minerals are classified based on their use and formation. For example, ferrous minerals like iron ore are essential for the electrical industry. Iron ore is mined in Bailadila and is a ferrous mineral. The finest iron ore with magnetic properties is considered the highest quality hard coal. Aluminium is obtained from this ore, and it is formed in veins and lodes. Additionally, minerals like those found in placer deposits and those formed due to evaporation are also important in the context of mineral resources.
