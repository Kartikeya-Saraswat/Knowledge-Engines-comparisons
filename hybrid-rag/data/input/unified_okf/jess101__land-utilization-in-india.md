# Land Utilization in India

Land resources are used for the following purposes:
1. Forests
2. Land not available for cultivation (a) Barren and waste land (b) Land put to non-agricultural uses, e.g., buildings, roads, factories, etc.
3. Other uncultivated land (excluding fallow land) (a) Permanent pastures and grazing land (b) Land under miscellaneous tree crops groves (not included in net sown area) (c) Culturable waste land (left uncultivated for more than 5 agricultural years)
4. Fallow lands (a) Current fallow (b) Other than current fallow
5. Net sown area - the physical extent of land on which crops are sown and harvested is known as net sown area.
