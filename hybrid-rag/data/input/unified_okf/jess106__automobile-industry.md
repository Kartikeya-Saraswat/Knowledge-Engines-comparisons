# Automobile Industry

Automobiles provide vehicle for quick transport of good services and passengers. Trucks, buses, cars, motor cycles, scooters, three-wheelers, and multi-utility vehicles are manufactured in India at various centres. After liberalization, the coming in of new and contemporary models stimulated the demand for vehicles in the market, leading to the healthy growth of the industry. The industry is located around Delhi, Gurugram, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata, Lucknow, Indore, Hyderabad, Jamshedpur, and Bengaluru.
