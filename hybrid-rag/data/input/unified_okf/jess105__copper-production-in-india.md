# Copper Production in India

India is critically deficient in copper reserves and production. Copper is used in electrical cables, electronics, and chemical industries. Leading copper mines include Balaghat in Madhya Pradesh, Khetri in Rajasthan, and Singhbhum in Jharkhand.
