# Water Scarcity and Its Causes

Water scarcity is caused by over-exploitation, excessive use, and unequal access to water among different social groups. It can also be due to poor water quality, pollution from domestic and industrial waste, and the use of chemicals, pesticides, and fertilizers in agriculture. These factors lead to declining groundwater levels and affect food security.
