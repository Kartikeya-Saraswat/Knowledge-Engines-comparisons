# Waterways and Inland Navigation

India has inland navigation waterways of 14,500 km in length. The National Waterways Act, 2016, declared 111 inland waterways as National Waterways (NWs). These include 5 National Waterways declared earlier. Inland water transport is considered an economical and environment-friendly supplementary mode of transport to rail and road.
