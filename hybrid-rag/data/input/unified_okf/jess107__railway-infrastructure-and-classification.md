# Railway Infrastructure and Classification

Railways in India are classified based on the type of material used for their construction. Metalled roads are made of cement, concrete, or bitumen of coal, and are weather-resistant. Unmetalled roads are no longer used in the rainy season. The Indian Railway is reorganised into 17 zones.
