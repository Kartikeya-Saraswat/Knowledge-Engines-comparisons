# Land Degradation and Conservation Measures

Land degradation is caused by human activities such as deforestation, overgrazing, mining, and quarrying. Mining sites leave deep scars and traces of over-burdening. Industrial effluents contribute to land and water pollution. Methods to combat land degradation include afforestation, proper grazing management, planting of shelter belts, and proper management of waste lands and industrial effluents.
