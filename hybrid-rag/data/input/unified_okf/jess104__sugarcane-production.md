# Sugarcane Production

Sugarcane is a tropical and subtropical crop, grown in regions with temperatures between 21°C and 27°C and annual rainfall between 75cm and 100cm. It requires irrigation in low rainfall areas. Major sugarcane-producing states include Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Punjab, and Haryana.
