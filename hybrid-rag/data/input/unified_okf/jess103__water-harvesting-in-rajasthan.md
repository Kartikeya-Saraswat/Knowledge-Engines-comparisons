# Water Harvesting in Rajasthan

In Rajasthan, water harvesting is practiced through various methods, including the use of underground tanks (tankas) and channels like kuls and guls. These systems are particularly common in areas like Bikaner, Phalodi, and Barmer, where houses are traditionally equipped with underground tanks for storing drinking water. The tanks can be as large as a big room, and in Phalodi, one household had a tank that was 6.1 meters deep, 4.27 meters long, and 2.44 meters wide. These tanks are part of a well-developed rooftop rainwater harvesting system that helps store water during dry periods.
