# Transport and its Importance

Transport is essential for the movement of goods and services, and it plays a vital role in economic development. Efficient means of transport are prerequisites for fast development. Transport, communication, and trade are complementary to each other. India is well-linked with the rest of the world despite its vast size, diversity, and linguistic and socio-cultural plurality. Modern means of transport and communication serve as lifelines of our nation and its modern economy.
