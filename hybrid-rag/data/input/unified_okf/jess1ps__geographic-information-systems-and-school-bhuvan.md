# Geographic Information Systems and School Bhuvan

School Bhuvan–NCERT has been launched by NCERT and ISRO to enhance geo-spatial skills among students. It includes thematic maps from Geography textbooks and enables students to use geospatial technology for understanding geographical concepts.
