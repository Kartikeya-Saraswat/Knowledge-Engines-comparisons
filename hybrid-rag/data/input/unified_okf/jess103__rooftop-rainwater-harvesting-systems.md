# Rooftop Rainwater Harvesting Systems

Rooftop rainwater harvesting systems are used to collect and store rainwater from rooftops for drinking and irrigation. These systems are particularly common in Rajasthan, where they are used to store drinking water in underground tanks called tankas. The systems involve collecting rainwater through PVC pipes, filtering it with sand and bricks, and storing it in underground tanks. In some areas, the water is recharged into the ground through wells, and the tanks are used for immediate consumption. In villages like Gendathur, rooftop rainwater harvesting systems are installed to meet water needs, with nearly 200 households benefiting from this system. The practice is also being successfully adapted in rural and urban areas.
