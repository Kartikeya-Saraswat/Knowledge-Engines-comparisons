# Soil Erosion and Its Causes

Soil erosion is the denudation of soil cover and subsequent washing down. It occurs simultaneously with soil formation and is balanced naturally. Human activities like deforestation, overgrazing, construction, and mining disturb this balance. Natural forces such as wind, glaciers, and water cause erosion. Water can create gullies, leading to land becoming unfit for cultivation. In the Chambal basin, such lands are called ravines. Sheet erosion occurs when water flows as a sheet over large areas, washing away soil. Wind erosion affects flat or sloping land, and defective farming methods like ploughing in the wrong way cause soil erosion. Contour ploughing and strip cropping help reduce erosion. Terrace farming restricts erosion in the Western and central Himalayas.
