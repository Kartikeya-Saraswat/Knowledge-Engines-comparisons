# Manufacturing Industries in India

Manufacturing industries in India are classified based on the source of raw materials and their main role. Agro-based industries use agricultural raw materials like cotton, jute, and silk, while mineral-based industries use resources like iron, steel, and cement. Industries are further categorized by capital investment, ownership, and the bulk of raw materials and finished goods. The textile industry is a key sector contributing to employment and foreign exchange, with cotton and jute being major examples. The classification helps in understanding the structure and role of these industries in the economy.
