# Pulses Production

India is the largest producer and consumer of pulses, which are a major source of protein in vegetarian diets. Major pulse-producing states include Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, and Karnataka. Pulses are leguminous crops that help restore soil fertility by fixing nitrogen.
