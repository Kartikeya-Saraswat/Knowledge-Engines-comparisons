# Soil Classification in India

India's soils are classified based on factors like colour, thickness, texture, age, and chemical/physical properties. The main soil types include alluvial, black, red and yellow, laterite, and arid soils. Alluvial soils are the most widespread and fertile, while black soils are ideal for cotton cultivation. Red and yellow soils form in low rainfall areas, and laterite soils develop under tropical climates. Arid soils are sandy and saline, and forest soils are found in hilly regions.
