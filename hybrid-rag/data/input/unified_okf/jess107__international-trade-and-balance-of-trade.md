# International Trade and Balance of Trade

International trade refers to the exchange of goods and services between countries. It includes export and import, with the balance of trade being the difference between export and import values. A favorable balance of trade occurs when exports exceed imports, while an unfavourable balance occurs when imports exceed exports.
