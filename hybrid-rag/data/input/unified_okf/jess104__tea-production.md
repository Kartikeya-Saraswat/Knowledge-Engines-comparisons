# Tea Production

Tea cultivation is an example of plantation agriculture. It is grown in tropical and sub-tropical climates with deep, fertile, well-drained soil. Major tea-producing states include Assam, Darjeeling, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, and Kerala. In 2020, India was the second largest tea producer after China.
