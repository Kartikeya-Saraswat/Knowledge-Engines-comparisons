# Mineral-Based Industries

Mineral-based industries are those that use minerals and metals as raw materials. Examples include the iron and steel industry, which relies on iron ore, coking coal, and lime stone in a ratio of approximately 4:2:1. Steel is essential for manufacturing various engineering goods, construction materials, defense, medical, telephonic, and scientific equipment, as well as consumer goods.
