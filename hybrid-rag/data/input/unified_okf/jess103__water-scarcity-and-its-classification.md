# Water Scarcity and Its Classification

The text discusses how regions are classified as 'suffering from water scarcity' or 'not suffering from water scarcity' based on factors like annual rainfall and population size. It highlights scenarios where high rainfall combined with large populations or high pollution can lead to water scarcity, even if the rainfall is high.
