# Wheat Distribution in India

Wheat is the second most important cereal crop in India. It is the main food crop in the north and northwest part of the country. It requires a cool growing season and bright sunshine at ripening. It requires 50 to 75 cm of annual rainfall evenly distributed. Major wheat-producing states are Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Bihar, and Rajasthan.
