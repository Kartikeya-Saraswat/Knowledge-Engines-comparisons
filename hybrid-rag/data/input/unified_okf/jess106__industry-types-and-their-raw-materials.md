# Industry Types and Their Raw Materials

The text explains different types of industries and their raw materials. For example, textile industries use raw materials like textiles, sugar, vegetable oil, and plantation industries derive raw materials from agriculture. The sugar industry uses sugar as its basic raw material. The steel industry uses iron-ore, coking coal, and limestone as its chief raw materials. A public sector steel plant is located in Chhattisgarh. Railway diesel engines are manufactured in Uttar Pradesh at a steel plant.
