# Environmental Management Programs

The JFM program in Odisha involves local communities in managing degraded forest lands, ensuring sustainable practices. Communities receive benefits like non-timber forest produce and shares in timber harvesting. These programs emphasize the importance of community involvement in natural resource management and highlight the role of local institutions in conservation efforts.
