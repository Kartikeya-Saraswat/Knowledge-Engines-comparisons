# Iron and Steel Industries

Iron and steel industries are concentrated in the Chhotanagpur plateau region due to factors like low-cost iron ore, high-grade raw materials in proximity, cheap labor, and vast growth potential in the home market. These industries are located in Odisha, West Bengal, Kerala, Uttar Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu.
