# Jute Production

Jute is used in making gunny bags, mats, ropes, yarn, carpets, and other artefacts. It is grown in well-drained fertile soils in flood plains, requiring high temperatures during growth. Major jute-producing states include West Bengal, Bihar, Assam, Odisha, and Meghalaya.
