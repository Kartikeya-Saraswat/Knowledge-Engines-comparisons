# Iron Ore Distribution in India

India has major iron ore belts: Odisha-Jharkhand, Durg-Bastar-Chandrapur, Ballari-Chitradurga-Chikkamagaluru-Tumakuru, and Maharashtra-Goa. The Odisha-Jharkhand belt includes hematite ores in Badampahar and Gua. The Durg-Bastar-Chandrapur belt has super high grade hematite in Bailadila. The Ballari-Chitradurga belt has large reserves in Kudremukh. The Maharashtra-Goa belt exports iron ore via Marmagao port.
