# Oil Seeds Production

India is the second largest producer of groundnut, with Gujarat being the largest producer. Major oil seeds include groundnut, mustard, coconut, sesamum (til), soyabean, castor seeds, cotton seeds, linseed, and sunflower. These are primarily used as cooking mediums and raw materials for soap, cosmetics, and ointments.
