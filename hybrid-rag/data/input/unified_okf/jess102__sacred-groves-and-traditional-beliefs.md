# Sacred Groves and Traditional Beliefs

Sacred groves are forests revered for their spiritual significance, where local communities protect them from deforestation. Tribals in India, such as the Mundas and Santhals, worship specific trees like mahua and kadamba, and these sacred groves are considered vital for ecological balance. The belief in sacredness extends to natural elements like springs and animals, which are treated as part of the community's heritage.
