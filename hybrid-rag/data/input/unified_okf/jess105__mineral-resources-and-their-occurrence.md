# Mineral Resources and Their Occurrence

Minerals are found in various forms and modes, such as in igneous and metamorphic rocks, sedimentary rocks, and alluvial deposits. They are usually found in ores, which are accumulations of minerals mixed with other elements. The occurrence of minerals depends on their geological formation, and this determines the ease of extraction and the cost. In India, minerals are found in different regions, with some areas having abundant reserves of specific minerals like iron ore, coal, and others.
