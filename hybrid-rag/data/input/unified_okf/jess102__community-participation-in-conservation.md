# Community Participation in Conservation

Local communities are actively involved in the management of unclassed forests, where they protect habitats and resist encroachment. The Chipko movement and joint forest management (JFM) programs exemplify community-led conservation efforts. Villagers in Rajasthan have successfully resisted mining and deforestation by invoking the Wildlife Protection Act and local rules. These initiatives highlight the importance of community involvement in preserving natural resources.
