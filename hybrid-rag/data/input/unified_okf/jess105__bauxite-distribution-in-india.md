# Bauxite Distribution in India

Bauxite is a clay-like substance that produces alumina and aluminum. India's bauxite deposits are mainly in Amarkantak plateau, Maikal hills, and Bilaspur-Katni plateau. Odisha was the largest bauxite-producing state, with Panchpatmali deposits in Koraput being the most important.
