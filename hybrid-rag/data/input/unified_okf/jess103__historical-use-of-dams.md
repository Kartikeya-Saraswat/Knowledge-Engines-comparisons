# Historical Use of Dams

Evidences of sophisticated irrigation works have been found in Kalinga (Odisha), Nagarjunakonda (Andhra Pradesh), Bennur (Karnataka), and Kolhapur (Maharashtra). During the time of Chandragupta Maurya, dams, lakes, and irrigation systems were extensively built.
