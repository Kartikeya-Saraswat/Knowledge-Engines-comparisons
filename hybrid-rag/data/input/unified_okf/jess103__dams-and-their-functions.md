# Dams and Their Functions

Dams are barriers across flowing water that impound rivers and rainwater for various uses. They are now referred to as multi-purpose projects where the many uses of the impounded water are integrated. For example, the Bhakra-Nangal project in the Sutluj-Beas river basin uses water for hydel power and irrigation. The Hirakud project in the Mahanadi basin integrates water conservation with flood control.
