# Steel Production and Consumption

Production and consumption of steel is often regarded as the index of a country’s development. Steel is a heavy industry due to the heavy and bulky nature of raw materials and finished goods, which entails high transportation costs. Iron ore, coking coal, and lime stone are required in a ratio of approximately 4:2:1. Manganese is also required to harden the steel. Steel plants should ideally be located near the raw material sources and with efficient transport networks for distribution.
