# Textile Industry

The textile industry is a vital part of India's economy, contributing significantly to industrial production, employment, and foreign exchange. Cotton textiles are produced using hand-spinning and handloom weaving techniques, while jute textiles are produced in West Bengal. The industry is classified based on the source of raw materials and their role in the production process. Cotton textiles are central to India's economy, and the textile industry supports various other industries through its demand for raw materials and by creating employment opportunities.
