# Rice Distribution in India

Rice is grown in the plains of north and northeastern India, coastal areas, and deltaic regions. Canal irrigation and tubewells have made it possible to grow rice in areas of less rainfall such as Punjab, Haryana, and western Uttar Pradesh and parts of Rajasthan.
