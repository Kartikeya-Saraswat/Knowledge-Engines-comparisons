# Types of Farming in India

India practices various types of farming, including primitive subsistence farming, intensive subsistence farming, and commercial farming. Primitive subsistence farming involves slash and burn methods, while intensive subsistence farming uses high doses of biochemical inputs and irrigation. Commercial farming involves the use of modern inputs like HYV seeds and chemical fertilizers, and examples include tea, coffee, and rubber plantations.
