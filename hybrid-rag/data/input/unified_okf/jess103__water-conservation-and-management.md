# Water Conservation and Management

Conservation and management of water resources are crucial for safeguarding health, ensuring food security, and preventing ecological degradation. The Jal Jeevan Mission aims to provide assured supply of potable water to rural households. It focuses on behavioral changes and smart water management in communities.
