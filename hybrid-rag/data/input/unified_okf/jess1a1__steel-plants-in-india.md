# Steel Plants in India

The following are steel plants in India:
1. IISCO (Indian Steel Company): Established in 1870-1913 in Burnpur (W.B.), initially private, later nationalized.
2. TISCO (Tata Steel Company): Established in 1907 in Jamshedpur (Jharkhand), owned by Jamshedji Tata.
3. VISL (Vishakhapatnam Steel Plant): Established in 1923 in Bhadravati (Karnataka), initially private as MISCO, later nationalized.
4. Bhilai Steel Plant: Established in 1959 in Bhilai (Chhattisgarh), public sector, located in Soviet Union.
5. Bokaro Steel Plant: Established in 1972 in Bokaro (Jharkhand), public sector, located in Soviet Union.
6. Durgapur Steel Plant: Established in 1959 in Durgapur (W.B.), public sector, located in the U.K.
7. Rourkela Steel Plant: Established in 1960 in Rourkela (Odisha), public sector, located in Germany.
8. Vishakhapatnam Steel Plant: Established in 1972 in Vishakhapatnam (Andhra Pradesh), public sector, located in India.
9. Salem Steel Plant: Established in 1972 in Salem (T. Nadu), public sector, located in India.
10. Vijay Nagar Steel Plant: Established in 1972 in Vijay Nagar (Karnataka), public sector, located in India.
