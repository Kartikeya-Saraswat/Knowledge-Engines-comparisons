# Manganese Production in India

Manganese is used in steel and ferro-manganese alloys. India's manganese production is not satisfactory, but it is used in bleaching powder, insecticides, and paints. The state-wise production of manganese is shown in Fig. 5.2, with Odisha contributing 16%, Karnataka 10%, Madhya Pradesh 33%, and Maharashtra 27%.
