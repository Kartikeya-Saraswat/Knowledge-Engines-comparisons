# Impact of Dams on Rivers and Ecosystems

Dams fragment rivers, making it difficult for aquatic fauna to migrate, especially for spawning. Reservoirs submerge vegetation and soil, leading to decomposition. Dams also trigger floods due to sedimentation and cause ecological issues like water-borne diseases and pests.
